WHAT IS ON THE TELLY

A song cut to the bar, and two minutes forty-seven of Jaguar: attract modes, gameplay, the homebrew from this repo, and the console shell with its eyes flaring on the beat. 854×480, 11.7 MB.

Every frame of footage came out of vjag_test. A script runs it with a stream of PPM frames into a fifo, ffmpeg reads the other end, and nothing raw touches the disk. Jaguar Hero and WIREWING are played on camera by their own test robots. The cuts land on a beat grid tracked from the song's stems.

Commercial games appear as footage only. No ROMs here.
